FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is blepharoplasty surgery?

Blepharoplasty is eyelid surgery that removes or repositions excess skin, muscle, and fat around the eyes. It can be done on the upper lids, the lower lids, or both, and is commonly chosen to correct droopy lids, hooded eyes, and under-eye bags.

Who is a good candidate for blepharoplasty surgery in NYC?

Good candidates are generally in good health, have realistic expectations, and are bothered by loose or heavy eyelid skin or puffiness around the eyes. Your surgeon will examine your eyes and medical history during a consultation to confirm whether the procedure is right for you.

What is the difference between upper and lower eyelid surgery?

Upper eyelid surgery reduces excess skin and fat above the eye, which can ease a heavy, hooded look. Lower eyelid surgery targets bags, puffiness, and loose skin beneath the eye. Some patients have one, and others combine both for a more balanced result.

How long is recovery after eyelid surgery?

Swelling and bruising are common at first and usually settle over the first couple of weeks, though healing varies from person to person. Your surgeon will give you specific aftercare instructions and a follow-up schedule so you know what to expect.
